## A calm place to publish

Mare Blog is a minimal, open-source blog built with Astro. It brings together
long-form **blog posts**, short **journal entries**, and standalone **pages**
in one lightweight site.

Pages are statically generated for fast loading. Find articles with built-in
full-text search, switch between light and dark themes, or follow new posts
through the [RSS feed](/rss.xml). Comments are powered by Giscus and GitHub
Discussions.

## Built with

This site uses [Astro](https://astro.build), [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com),
[Pagefind](https://pagefind.app), and [Giscus](https://giscus.app).

## Make it yours

You can deploy your own copy of Mare Blog to Cloudflare Pages and customize it.

[![Deploy to Cloudflare Pages](https://img.shields.io/badge/Deploy%20to-Cloudflare%20Pages-F38020?logo=cloudflare&logoColor=white)](https://dash.cloudflare.com/?to=/:account/pages/new/provider/github)

Follow the [setup and configuration guide](/blog/guide/) to configure your
site, publish content, and connect Giscus.

The project is open source: [view the source on GitHub](https://github.com/Niceeepoiu/mare-blog).
